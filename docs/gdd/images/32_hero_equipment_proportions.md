## Image-generation brief

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Illustration 32 — Hero and Equipment Proportions, for direct insertion in the Erelia Game Design Document.
Composition/framing: 2:1 landscape.
Style/medium: grayscale graphite and gray-pencil game-design sketch on very light paper, controlled visible strokes, restrained hatching, clear silhouettes and generous paper margins. Voxel-fantasy terrain and architecture; Heroes have the block-bodied, cube-headed proportions used in the later Erelia illustrations. No color, glossy rendering, photograph, watermark, decorative poster headings or dense explanatory paragraphs.
Global constraints: classless Heroes; no levels, experience, professions, Feats, class badges, Mana/Energy/Rage. Three active Heroes per player whenever a full squad is shown. Permanent voxel terrain is immutable. Equipment Types and Tags are semantic properties, with no automatic effects. AP pays for combat actions and spells; MP pays for tactical movement. Eight equipped spells per Hero. Readiness may be drawn, but never an Encounter Clock HUD timer. Do not imply new established mechanics or fixed numeric defaults.
Primary request: Create a grayscale voxel-fantasy character reference-sheet proposal. Left third: front, side and back views of ONE blank Hero with a cube-shaped head, articulated block-shaped limbs and clearly readable hands and feet, in a simple neutral tunic. Match identical body proportions across the three views, with no nudity or anatomy labels. Show subtle attachment-point indicators for head, chest, legs, boots, hands and one accessory; accessory-slot count remains configurable. Right two thirds: the SAME Hero identity and body wearing three equipment combinations, with no class labels: sword plus shield, a two-handed staff with both hands occupied and no shield, and a two-handed axe with both hands occupied and no shield. Loose small equipment studies clarify how armor and gear attach to the body; no leveling progression or class transformation. Proportions and silhouettes are a proposed visual direction, not a final requirement. Short labels only Head, Chest, Legs, Boots, Hands, Accessory.
