## Image-generation brief

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Illustration 27 — Equipment Lifecycle, for direct insertion in the Erelia Game Design Document.
Composition/framing: 2.2:1 landscape.
Style/medium: grayscale graphite and gray-pencil game-design sketch on very light paper, controlled visible strokes, restrained hatching, clear silhouettes and generous paper margins. Voxel-fantasy terrain and architecture; Heroes have the block-bodied, cube-headed proportions used in the later Erelia illustrations. No color, glossy rendering, photograph, watermark, decorative poster headings or dense explanatory paragraphs.
Global constraints: classless Heroes; no levels, experience, professions, Feats, class badges, Mana/Energy/Rage. Three active Heroes per player whenever a full squad is shown. Permanent voxel terrain is immutable. Equipment Types and Tags are semantic properties, with no automatic effects. AP pays for combat actions and spells; MP pays for tactical movement. Eight equipped spells per Hero. Readiness may be drawn, but never an Encounter Clock HUD timer. Do not imply new established mechanics or fixed numeric defaults.
Primary request: Create a compact horizontal object-story sketch of ONE identifiable sword with a distinctive angular crossguard. Left to right: a crafting anvil produces the new sword; a Hero equips it; later the same sword is worn and its small durability bar has decreased; finally at zero durability the item has vanished and the equipment slot is empty. At the worn state, a small repair-anvil inset has a loop arrow returning toward continued use, showing that repair delays breakage and does not make the item permanent. A faint looping production arrow leads from the empty slot back toward new crafting. Clearly show disappearance: no persistent broken sword, dropped fragments, salvage reward or repairable zero-durability object at the final state. Do not specify repair costs, maximum-durability degradation, loss triggers or exact values. Short labels only Craft, Equip, Use, Repair, Gone.
