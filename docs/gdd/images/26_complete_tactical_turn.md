Style/medium: grayscale graphite and gray-pencil game-design sketch on very light paper, controlled visible strokes, restrained hatching, clear silhouettes and generous paper margins. Voxel-fantasy terrain and architecture; Heroes have the block-bodied, cube-headed proportions used in the later Erelia illustrations. No color, glossy rendering, photograph, watermark, decorative poster headings or dense explanatory paragraphs.

Eight equipped spells per Hero. Readiness may be drawn, but never an Encounter Clock HUD timer. Do not imply new established mechanics or fixed numeric defaults.
Primary request: Draw one hand-sketched proposed tactical gameplay view, not a sequence or dense infographic. High-angle view of a small elevated voxel battlefield with stairs and a blocking wall, exactly three Heroes and two enemies on actual terrain-derived combat cells. The selected Hero has a clear cell outline. Show a short two-cell movement preview and a separately indicated valid spell target cell. A skeletal interface at the bottom has the selected Hero portrait, an HP bar, AP as four small action-point pips, MP as three movement-point pips, exactly EIGHT spell icon cards arranged as two rows of four, one selected spell icon, and an End Turn control. At one margin, tiny unit portraits have readiness tracks; the selected Hero is fully Ready. Visually distinguish movement-path arrows from targeting lines using pencil weight and hatching. All pip counts are illustrative UI examples, not game balance defaults. Keep labels only HP, AP, MP, Ready, End Turn. No internal clock or numbered character levels.
