## Image-generation brief

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Illustration 33 — First World at Gameplay Scale, for direct insertion in the Erelia Game Design Document.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape.
Style/medium: grayscale graphite and gray-pencil game-design sketch on very light paper, controlled visible strokes, restrained hatching, clear silhouettes and generous paper margins. Voxel-fantasy terrain and architecture; Heroes have the block-bodied, cube-headed proportions used in the later Erelia illustrations. No color, glossy rendering, photograph, watermark, decorative poster headings or dense explanatory paragraphs.
Global constraints: classless Heroes; no levels, experience, professions, Feats, class badges, Mana/Energy/Rage. Three active Heroes per player whenever a full squad is shown. Permanent voxel terrain is immutable. Equipment Types and Tags are semantic properties, with no automatic effects. AP pays for combat actions and spells; MP pays for tactical movement. Eight equipped spells per Hero. Readiness may be drawn, but never an Encounter Clock HUD timer. Do not imply new established mechanics or fixed numeric defaults.
Primary request: Draw one representative first-World concept scene from a practical THIRD-PERSON camera just behind and above the controlled Hero, at gameplay distance rather than a panoramic aerial view. Exactly THREE block-bodied Heroes: one controlled Hero close to foreground center and two physical followers slightly farther behind to the sides, all visible. Terrain-scale references include knee-high slab steps, a traversable stair slope, a cliff edge, a normal-width stone bridge and trees several Hero-heights tall. Resources are separate ore deposits and harvestable wood nodes placed on immutable ground; no mining terrain. In the medium distance, one roaming enemy can be seen near a route. In the background, a permanent town silhouette and a dungeon entrance serve as navigable landmarks. Emphasize readable silhouettes and line of travel at actual player scale, not detailed panoramic architecture. Biome, anatomy, materials and architecture are proposals. No tactical grid, HUD, labels, quest icons, minimap or floating level numbers.
