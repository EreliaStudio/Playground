## Image-generation brief

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Illustration 28 — Expedition Inventory and Global Bank, for direct insertion in the Erelia Game Design Document.
Composition/framing: 2:1 landscape.
Style/medium: grayscale graphite and gray-pencil game-design sketch on very light paper, controlled visible strokes, restrained hatching, clear silhouettes and generous paper margins. Voxel-fantasy terrain and architecture; Heroes have the block-bodied, cube-headed proportions used in the later Erelia illustrations. No color, glossy rendering, photograph, watermark, decorative poster headings or dense explanatory paragraphs.
Global constraints: classless Heroes; no levels, experience, professions, Feats, class badges, Mana/Energy/Rage. Three active Heroes per player whenever a full squad is shown. Permanent voxel terrain is immutable. Equipment Types and Tags are semantic properties, with no automatic effects. AP pays for combat actions and spells; MP pays for tactical movement. Eight equipped spells per Hero. Readiness may be drawn, but never an Encounter Clock HUD timer. Do not imply new established mechanics or fixed numeric defaults.
Primary request: Create a restrained relationship sketch. On the left exactly THREE identifiable Heroes stand together; all connect to ONE small shared expedition inventory grid, not three inventories. The grid has raw resources, a potion, a spare sword and a tangible tradable spell object represented by a small inscribed tablet. In the middle ONE bank chest represents the player's global bank. On the right three tiny locations are connected to that very same bank chest: Town A, Town B, and one active crystal Outpost with storage service. Use bidirectional thin connector arrows between the single carried inventory and bank access, and from each service location to the same bank. No separate bank balances, private bank copies, transport carts between towns, or implied access anywhere in the wilderness. Limited short labels only Expedition Inventory, Global Bank, Town A, Town B, Outpost. Objects depict their roles; the exact spell-object appearance is a proposal.
